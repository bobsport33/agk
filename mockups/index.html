What changed

The layouts now have three different organizing ideas: a typographic poster, an exhibition of campaigns, and an editorial collage. Color occupies full surfaces. Scale, image placement and composition carry the personality.

The home pages show the actual creative work, then connect freelance work and children’s books to the same person. Each concept has working navigation and an audience-specific contact demonstration.

The Gallery’s three campaign controls change the featured image and its case-study link. The Poster and Playbook use deliberate image sequencing and responsive compositions. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
